# HKBU MCCP6010 Why Does TikTok Know What You Like

Group 6 presents a 15-minute lesson on recommendation algorithms.

## Teaching materials

- [Teaching script and presenter operations](TikTok_Lesson_Complete_Script_and_Operations.docx): spoken English and sentence-level cues for all 45 main lesson Parts and the six optional on-screen quiz Parts. Presenter actions use red text on fluorescent yellow backgrounds.
- [Student learning guide](For_You_Group6_Student_Learning_Guide.docx): a four-page worksheet with matching page and Part references, activity instructions, calculation spaces and reflection questions.

## Online lesson

[Open the interactive lesson](https://xanderzhou2022.github.io/HKBU_MCCP6010_Why-does-TikTok-know-what-you-like/)

The `gh-pages` branch contains the self-contained `index.html` and `.nojekyll`. Fonts, illustrations and scripts are embedded in the page.

## Presenting

- Press **Right** or **Space** to advance exactly one Part, and **Left** to return to the previous Part. The footer identifies the current page and Part, such as `08 - Part 4`.
- Press **O** to choose a specific page and Part, **F** for fullscreen, and **N** for the current Part's spoken lines and highlighted operation cues. Notes are visible on the current screen; close them before projecting.
- Click a countdown marked **Start** to begin. Click **Pause** or **Resume** as needed. Opening a page, navigating, returning to an activity or resetting never starts a countdown.
- Each Part fits the lesson viewport. Shared content stays in place between Parts.
- Set separate Kahoot host and participant links in Presentation settings, or select **Use the on-screen quiz instead** at `11 - Part 1`.

## Quiz routes

With Kahoot, pressing Right after the quiz moves from `11 - Part 1` to the takeaways at `11 - Part 2`.

Selecting the on-screen quiz inserts six Parts: question 1 at Part 2, answer 1 at Part 3, question 2 at Part 4, answer 2 at Part 5, question 3 at Part 6 and answer 3 at Part 7. The takeaways then appear at `11 - Part 8`. Right follows this whole sequence; choosing an answer on screen is optional.
